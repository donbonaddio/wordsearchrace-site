Privacy Policy

Word Search Race · Last updated November 2026

Word Search Race collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We don’t run a server, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, and we never send your data to us or to any third party.

Your game data

Your settings, Daily results, streak, and best scores are saved only on your own device using Apple’s standard on-device storage. We have no access to them. Deleting the app removes them.

Games in Messages

When you race someone in Messages, the game travels inside your own iMessage conversation: the theme, the grid’s number, and each player’s score. It is sent by Apple’s Messages like any other message. We never see it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple’s Game Center, your Daily scores and streak are posted to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy; we receive nothing from it. You can sign out in Settings › Game Center.

Children

Word Search Race is suitable for all ages and, because it collects no data at all, it does not gather information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
